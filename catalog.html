WebHemi chrome catalog

Owned Win98 control surface (assets/style/chrome/). Product pane layouts (icon / wizard / heading / dialog) are intentionally omitted. Classes catalog-active and catalog-focused simulate pressed and focus looks that cannot stay in the real :active / :focus states.
